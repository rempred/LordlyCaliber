**Lordly Caliber v0.5.6 — Attack Playback Fixes**

- **Battle softlocks:** Fixed late spell impacts leaving playback stuck on a completed wait. Long effects could also disrupt the timing of the next attack, causing it to be skipped or processed incorrectly.
- **Damage disappearing when adding status effects:** Additional effects such as Sleep or Paralysis could overwrite pending damage information. The attack calculated damage correctly, but that damage never reached the target during playback.
- **Missing secondary results:** Ordinary spells could apply their main result while silently dropping an additional result, including instant defeat. Both now resolve at the appropriate impact.
- **Drain and recoil not applying:** Custom drain or recoil could calculate correctly but leave the attacker's HP unchanged because the selected visual never produced a caster impact. These results now apply after the main impacts.
- **Physical healing and instant defeat:** These effects could be calculated without being applied during physical attack playback. They now resolve at the attack's scheduled impact.
- **Status effects applying in the wrong order:** An attack that applied a status and then removed it could leave the status active because the primary animation finished late. For example, petrification followed by a cure now finishes with the character cured.
- **Missing defeat reactions:** Multiple damage results at the same impact could reduce HP to zero without triggering the defeat reaction. Later results now account for damage already dealt by that impact.
- **Automated regression testing:** Added a playback harness with reproducible generated combinations. Passed 4,250 cases covering physical attacks, spells, combined spells, Pedra attacks, repeated strikes, status effects, drain, recoil and following actions.
